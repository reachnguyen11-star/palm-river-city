# Palm River - Website Conversion Content

## Muc Tieu Trang

Bien thong diep chu dau tu **"Do thi nghi duong giua long thanh pho"** thanh noi dung website co kha nang chuyen doi cho 2 nhom khach:

- **Khach mua de o:** can moi truong song, rieng tu, tien ich, gia tri cho gia dinh.
- **Khach dau tu:** can vi tri, tinh khan hiem, benchmark phan khuc, thanh khoan va kha nang khai thac.

Trang nen ban theo logic:

**Cam xuc ve chuan song -> Bang chung ve quy hoach/tien ich -> Ly do tai san dang gia -> CTA tu van theo muc tieu mua.**

## 15. Luu Y Claim

Dung manh neu co tai lieu chinh thuc:

- 100% can ho co cho dau o to.
- So luong thang may va mat do can/san.
- Phong gia nhan trong layout nao.
- Tien ich Sky Onsen, Cold Plunge, ho boi 70m, ho boi vo cuc.
- Thoi gian ban giao, phap ly, ngan hang bao lanh, nha thau.

Dung an toan neu chua co tai lieu:

- "Theo thong tin thi truong/tai lieu ban hang..."
- "Du kien..."
- "Huong den..."
- "Co the khai thac..."
- "Nam trong tap tham chieu..."

Khong nen dung:

- Cam ket sinh loi.
- Tang gia chac chan.
- Tot hon The Define/The Metropole/Estella Heights/Dao Kim Cuong.
- Phap ly 100% neu chua co ho so doi chieu.
- Ga metro truoc nha neu chua co nguon quy hoach ro rang.
